About Us

Founded in 2005 by Rosemary Turk, Octel Networks began as a specialized network infrastructure provider serving businesses across Bengaluru. After recognizing that clients needed both technical depth and end-to-end accountability, we evolved into a full-service network and IT infrastructure partner.

Today, we serve 200+ clients across enterprise, retail, and mall environments with a team of network engineers, field technicians, and infrastructure specialists, delivering solutions across LAN/WAN, SD-WAN, surveillance, unified communications, and managed IT operations.
