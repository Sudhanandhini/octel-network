From the first assessment to ongoing support, you get a single point of contact and 24x7 assistance, so every project is delivered and run as one connected environment.

PUBLIC SECTOR & GOVERNMENT PROCUREMENT

Supplying government departments through the Government e-Marketplace (GeM)

Octel Networks bids for government tenders through GeM, India’s national online portal for public procurement. It gives government departments and public sector organizations a transparent, documented way to source network and IT infrastructure from a partner they can verify.

Each bid follows the discipline of our enterprise projects: requirements read closely, solutions specified accurately, pricing kept clear. When an order is awarded, our own engineers deliver, install and support it, with the same single point of contact and 24x7 assistance as any other Octel client.
