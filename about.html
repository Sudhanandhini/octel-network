Founded in 2005 as a specialized IT infrastructure reseller serving small businesses in Chicago, Octel Networks began with a simple mission: deliver enterprise-grade networking equipment with dependable technical support. As client networks grew more complex, we evolved into a full-service infrastructure distributor and solutions provider.

Today, we serve 200+ clients nationwide with a team of 15 network engineers and infrastructure specialists, delivering authorized distribution for Rittal, R&M, and NETKA System.
