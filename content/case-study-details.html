FastTrack Apparel

Online Retail, USA · 6-month engagement

E-Commerce Efficiency Boost

FastTrack Apparel came to us processing over 400 orders a day across three sales channels, but reconciling revenue at month-end took nearly two weeks — by which point the numbers were already stale.

The Challenge

Disorganized finances and inaccurate forecasting were costing FastTrack real money: inventory purchasing decisions were being made on gut feel, and a cash crunch nearly delayed a key supplier payment.

The Solution

  • Migrated all three sales channels into a single cloud accounting system
  • Built live KPI dashboards for daily revenue, margin and cash position
  • Automated month-end reconciliation, cutting close time from 14 days to 2
  • Introduced a rolling 13-week cash-flow forecast reviewed every Monday
“We finally make inventory decisions based on real numbers, not guesswork. Exrox gave us that visibility inside of six weeks.”

The Outcome

Within two quarters, FastTrack cut accounting errors by 40%, improved cash-flow forecasting accuracy by 25%, and grew quarterly profits by 15% — largely by catching margin leaks the old reporting simply couldn't see.
