OUR HERITAGE

Vijaya Kumar Raju founded Octel Networks in 1999 as a specialist network infrastructure provider in Bengaluru. Our clients wanted deep technical expertise backed by complete accountability, so we grew into a full-service infrastructure partner. Today more than 15,000 clients across enterprise, retail and mall environments rely on us.

What we deliver

Our capabilities cover the full infrastructure stack. In networking, we deliver LAN/WAN, SD-WAN, enterprise Wi-Fi and structured cabling. Our cybersecurity practice extends to air-gapped environments for high-security operations. We also design and integrate data centre and cloud infrastructure, and we provide network management and monitoring for proactive visibility and performance control. Surveillance, unified communications and managed IT operations complete the portfolio, so clients can rely on a single partner from build through day-to-day operations.
