Octel Networks Pvt Ltd ("Octel Networks", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1Who We Are

Octel Networks Pvt Ltd is the data controller (data fiduciary) responsible for your personal information. Our head office is at 887 11th Main 4th Cross HAL 2nd Stage, Bangalore, Karnataka, India - 560008. You can reach us about privacy at info@octelnetworks.com.

2Information We Collect

We only collect information that you give us or that is needed to run the website:

  • Enquiries: when you use our contact or consultation forms, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the type of enquiry (sales or support) and your message.
  • Job applications: when you apply through our Careers page, we collect your name, email address, phone number, qualification, experience, cover letter and the CV / resume you upload.
  • Communications: emails, calls and messages you exchange with our team, including chat messages sent through the website chat assistant.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our server automatically records basic technical details such as your IP address, browser type, pages requested and the date and time of your visit, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Cookie choices: your cookie preferences, stored on your device (see Cookies).

We do not ask for sensitive personal data (such as health, religion or biometric data) through our website. Please do not include it in your messages.

4Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies, which the website needs to work, such as remembering your cookie choice and protecting forms. These do not need consent.
  • Analytics cookies, which help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Used only if you allow them.
  • Marketing cookies, which help us show relevant content and measure campaigns. Used only if you allow them.

When you first visit, we ask for your consent. Optional cookies stay off unless you choose to allow them. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the cookie button at the bottom left of every page, or here: Cookie Settings. Your choice is remembered for 180 days.

5Sharing Your Information

We share personal information only when needed, and only with:

  • Service providers who host our website, email and IT systems for us, under contracts that require them to protect your data and use it only on our instructions.
  • Manufacturers and partners we represent (such as Rittal, R&M, NETKA System and Matrix), where needed to quote for, supply or support the products you ask about.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, such as auditors, lawyers, regulators or law enforcement, when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6International Transfers

We are based in India, so information you send us is processed in India. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, this means your data is transferred outside your country. Where required, we protect such transfers with appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or rely on the transfer being necessary to answer your request.

7How Long We Keep It

  • Enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer.
  • Customer records: for as long as we serve you, and afterwards for the period required by tax and accounting laws.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months after the hiring decision, unless you are hired or ask us to keep your CV for future roles.
  • Server logs: for a limited period needed for security, normally no more than 12 months.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to admin systems, and access only for staff who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a data breach where the law requires.

9Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase your information when we no longer need it or you withdraw consent.
  • Restrict or object to how we use your information, including for direct marketing.
  • Data portability: receive your information in a common format or have it sent to another organisation.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to (India).
  • Complain to a data protection authority, such as the supervisory authority in your EU country, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email info@octelnetworks.com. We may need to confirm your identity, and we will reply within one month. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern before you contact an authority.

10Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11Third-Party Links & Services

Our website contains links to other websites, such as our partners' sites and our social media pages, and shows embedded Google Maps on the Contact page. These services have their own privacy policies and may collect information when you use them. We are not responsible for their practices, so please review their policies.

12Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date at the top. If we make significant changes, we will let you know on the website or, where appropriate, by email.
